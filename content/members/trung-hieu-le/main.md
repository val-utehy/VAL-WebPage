Dr. Trung-Hieu Le is a lecturer in the Faculty of Information Technology at Hung Yen University of Technology and Education, Viet Nam. From 2020 to 2023, he was a Research Assistant Professor at National Taipei University of Technology, Taiwan.

His research focuses on image processing and computer vision applications with machine learning and deep learning. He has authored and co-authored research published by leading IEEE journals and Academic Press.
